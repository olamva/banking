# Privacy Policy — Personal Spending Analysis

**Last updated: 8 August 2026**

This is a personal, single-user application. It is not offered to the public
and has no users other than its author.

## What it does

It reads the author's own bank account and credit card information —
account details, balances and transactions — from the author's own bank,
with the author's own consent given through the bank's authentication.

## What it does with the data

Data is fetched to the author's personal computer and stored there. It is
used to produce monthly spending summaries for the author's own use.

- No data is transmitted to any third party.
- No data is published, sold, shared or used for advertising or profiling.
- No other person's data is accessed, since only the author's own accounts
  are connected.
- No servers, hosting or cloud storage are involved. The application runs
  locally and stores its files on a single machine.

## Retention

Data is kept locally for as long as the author finds it useful and is
deleted by deleting the local files. Access can be revoked at any time
through the bank or by deleting the application's credentials.
